# adidas-us-sales-dashboard

An interactive Excel analytics dashboard designed to track, visualize, and analyze Adidas US sales performance, regional distributions, and consumer purchasing behavior.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
<img width="946" height="377" alt="photo p" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/170c44bd-171c-4f0d-861a-81ffa13394b2" />


The **Adidas US Interactive Sales Dashboard** provides a holistic, high-level view of retail operations, product demand, and financial performance across three fiscal years (2023–2025). Built to transform raw transactional data into actionable commercial insights, the dashboard is divided into three core analytical zones:

### 1. High-Level KPIs
* **Total Sales:** $287,377.92
* **Total Profit:** $85,070
* **Total Units Sold:** 3,550

### 2. Core Visual Insights
* **Yearly Sales Trend:** Line chart mapping a macro-level performance overview from 2023 ($107,348) through 2025 ($76,800), highlighting shifting revenue trajectories.
* **Total by Categories:** Breakdown of inventory categories showing Accessories taking a dominant **42%** share, followed by Apparel (**29%**) and Footwear (**29%**).
* **Order by Payment Method:** Column chart assessing consumer transaction preferences, led by **PayPal** ($42,349) and **NetBanking** ($39,118).
* **Order by Store Type:** Donut chart evaluating fulfillment channels, pinpointing **Online** (549 orders) and **Retail** (422 orders) as top volume drivers.
* **Top 5 Products:** Horizontal bar chart spotlighting best-sellers, led by the **Stan Smith** (76 units sold) and the **Superstar** (73 units sold).

### 3. Dynamic Slicers & Interactivity
The dashboard includes an interactive control panel allowing stakeholders to seamlessly drill down and filter the entire report by:
* **Region:** (e.g., Egypt, KSA, Oman, Iraq, Lebanon)
* **Store Type:** (Online, Outlet, Retail, Wholesale)
* **Gender:** (Female, Male, Other)
* **Order Date:** Granular time-series filter spanning 2023, 2024, and 2025.
